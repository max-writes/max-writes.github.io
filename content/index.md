Hello! I am Max and this is where I write about the things I'm interested in. Review my [Now](now.md) page to see what I'm up to lately. I am a creative soul, writer, philosopher, and designer. A digital gardener, zine-maker, and chronicler of small moments. My life is [a manifesto of process and presence](slightly-polished/manifesto-of-process-and-presence.md).

## <span class="sketch-highlight">Purpose</span>

Build slow archive of things I notice and think about. [Learning in public](slightly-polished/learning-in-public.md) through my [digital garden](thoughts/digital-gardens.md) helps me document and share my evolving thoughts, get over perfectionism, and build a personal knowledge archive that grows with me. 

<span class="text green-mark">A goal for this site is to capture the paths of learning and growth that I take. </span>

Proceed knowing that I  write about a variety of topics and I know I could be wrong! When I talk about my life and events I try to bring it back a few steps to generalize the experience, but if I've misrepresented anything concerning, please let me know and I'll correct it.

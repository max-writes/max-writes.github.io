A slow archive of things I notice and think about.

**This is in progress and not fully built/styled.  I am in the process of migrating stuff from my old garden system so there will be some formatting issues too.**

Hello! I am Max and this is where I write about the things I'm interested in. Review my [Now](now.md) page to see what I'm up to lately. I am a creative soul, writer, philosopher, and designer. I am a digital gardener, zine-maker, and chronicler of small moments. I build intentional digital spaces with texture, not just a place to store content. My creativity exists somewhere between high-precision and messy friction. My life is [a manifesto of process and presence](slightly-polished/manifesto-of-process-and-presence.md).

As a technical communicator, I specialize in humanizing complex systems. Documentation is a user experience; I seek to help users navigate technology with confidence!

**Purpose:** [Learning in public](slightly-polished/learning-in-public.md) through my [digital garden](thoughts/digital-gardens.md) helps me document and share my evolving thoughts, get over perfectionism, and build a personal knowledge archive that grows with me. My goal for this site is to create non-linear paths of capturing learning and growth. 

**Disclaimer:** Proceed knowing that I  write about a variety of topics and I know I could be wrong! When I talk about my life and events I try to bring it back a few steps to generalize the experience, but if I've misrepresented anything concerning, please let me know and I'll correct it.

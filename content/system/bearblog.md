## About Bearblog

<a href="https://bearblog.dev" target="_blank">Bearblog</a> is a minimalist blogging platform built around speed, accessibility, and minimalism, focusing on written text. I have a paid account so I can post photos and make multiple blogs.

* **Why I started:** I was keeping another blog on Neocities for the "nostalgia" which I didn't want to close, but which wasn't easy enough for me to update on the go. Flat and minimal blog platforms popup all the time; I picked this one because customization is CSS-based and I didn't want to deal with many template files.

* **Why I continued:** Maintaining separate, topic-focused blogs helps me declutter my brain. The system worked for me, so I stayed and made more blogs.

* **What it offers me/value:** I can write full blog posts from my phone while I'm out & about without cluttering my notetaking app (I hate writing drafts there, it's not meant for longform writing). 

## Learning Bearblog

* **Help articles:** Reference the <a href="https://docs.bearblog.dev/" target="_blank">Bear Docs</a> for help with getting set up, customization, and other needs.

* **Getting started:** It's very easy to set up and get started. I recommend having a purpose for your blog (even if it's freewrite or not niche like mine are) because that helps you keep going with it.

## Bearblog flows

I have a few blogs, each dedicated to a specific theme, update cadence, and workflow.

### Seasons

<a href="https://seasons.bearblog.dev/" target="_blank">Visit Seasons</a>

Reflections and personal thoughts about passing seasons and regional environment. I try to update a few times each month to record seasonal shifts and changing cycles.

### Where Were We?

<a href="https://wherewerewe.bearblog.dev/" target="_blank">Visit Where Were We?</a>

Photo highlights from my camera roll. I try to update monthly (it doesn't always happen) to create a visual blog of my life and experiences.

### Chatscape

<a href="https://heychat.bearblog.dev/" target="_blank">Visit Chatscape</a>

Thoughts and reactions on AI/LLMs, plus a repository of AI data settings and instructions. I usually let thoughts percolate for a while, then draft/update a bunch of things at once after observing/reading how people are processing the impacts of this technology.

### Gamezilla

<a href="https://gamezilla.bearblog.dev/" target="_blank">Visit GAMEZILLA</a>

Notes and casual write-ups about whatever I've been playing lately. Updated sporadically when the mood strikes and I often forget about it.
